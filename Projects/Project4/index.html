Data Protection Principles

Privacy & Security Standards

The "Unakku Avlotha Limit" prototype adheres to strict data minimization, non-disclosure, and internal tokenization architecture.

1. Identity Tokenization & Zero Public Exposure

Real 12-digit Aadhaar numbers are never stored in plain text or shared with public interfaces. The platform uses pseudonymous internal User IDs (e.g., TN-RES-1001) and masks all credentials (e.g., XXXX-XXXX-7812) to prevent identity theft.

2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Administrative modules containing enforcement notes, case references, and slot configurations are strictly isolated. Normal residents can only query their own active quota and booking ledger. Cross-user inspection is strictly impossible.

3. Sensitive Legal Restriction Protection

If a court or police incident restriction is recorded against an individual, normal citizen views only see:

“Your purchase access is currently restricted. Please contact the appropriate authority for further information.”

Confidential FIR numbers, court orders, and officer remarks are exclusively accessible to authorized administrators.

4. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP Act) Alignment

Designed under the principles of the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, ensuring purposeful limitation, secure audit trails, right to erasure upon lawful clearance, and zero commercial advertising.
