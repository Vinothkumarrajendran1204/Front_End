Core Architecture

Disciplined Booking, Zero Crowding, Absolute Health Awareness

A student demonstration model for modernizing retail operations and upholding public safety across Tamil Nadu.

🔒

Daily Limit Enforcement

Centralized daily quotas tied to masked Aadhaar records prevent bulk purchasing. HOT is capped at 1 unit/day and NON-HOT at 2 units/day.

📱

Digital QR Pass System

Generates offline scannable QR tokens (UAL-2026-XXXX). Customers arrive only during their reserved 1-hour collection slot, eliminating long street queues.

🧠

Public Health Education

Interactive "Know the Effects" diagrams comparing life without alcohol vs. alcohol use, interactive organ anatomy, and 24/7 helplines (Tele-MANAS 14416).
