Manapparai

Manapparai is an assembly constituency located in the Tiruchirappalli district of Tamil Nadu. It is designated as Assembly Constituency No. 138 and falls under the Karur Lok Sabha constituency for parliamentary elections.

Manapparai is a municipality in Tamil Nadu's Tiruchirappalli district, located about 38 km from Trichy on the Trichy-Dindigul National Highway. It is globally renowned for its GI-tagged Manapparai Murukku and its historic cattle market, as well as the Sri Nallandavar Temple and Mallam Subramanya Swamy Temple.
