What is IT

"IT" (Information Technology) is the use of computer systems, networks, and software to store, process, and secure digital data. It is the backbone of modern operations, combining physical hardware, applications, and telecommunications to make global communication and data management possible.


What is Service-Based Company

A service-based company generates revenue by providing intangible expertise, labor, or specialized skills to other businesses or individual clients rather than selling physical goods. Examples include IT consulting firms, marketing agencies, and legal practices.


What is Product-Based Company

A product-based company designs, manufactures, and sells its own physical or digital goods to consumers or businesses. Instead of completing one-off projects for specific clients, they continuously invest in research, development, and improvement of their proprietary offerings to drive scalable revenue.
