Built on Trust & Radical Transparency

CommitCode is an educational accountability platform, not an investment scheme or loan product. We clearly state all conditions upfront.

1. Defined Completion Criteria

Refund eligibility requires 100% of defined course tasks to be submitted within their 24h windows (or approved permissions) and approved by staff.

2. Upfront Disclosure

Every course displays its exact syllabus, number of tasks, video hours, tuition fee, and maximum refund ceiling before enrollment.

3. Objective Staff Reviews

All submissions are graded against standardized industry rubrics and test suites. Transparent revision feedback is provided on non-compliant code.

4. Clear Permission Rules

Permissions are non-transferable tokens designed exclusively for 24-hour deadline extensions or excused emergency periods.

5. Course-Specific Refund Caps

Completion refunds are capped at up to 50% depending on the specific program's operational costs, with exact rupee limits published on every card.

6. Regulatory Compliance

The platform does not offer guaranteed income, investment returns, or financial loans. Eligibility is subject to course completion requirements and Refund Policy.
